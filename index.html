How pricing works

Price list, or a quote

Price varies depending on size, quantity and design. Flower bouquets and the picture bouquet are priced from the shop's price list, so you pay at checkout. Money, makeup, sweets, diaper and beer gifts are quoted from the materials and the labour of assembly — and from the items themselves only if the shop has to buy them.
